Workflow 1

Keyword & Ranking Gap Analysis

Tool: Cerebro

Pull competitor ASIN keywords and identify gaps in your own listings — keywords they rank for that you don't.

Steps

  1. Give Claude your own ASIN via Helium 10 MCP and ask it to find the top 2-3 competitor ASINs in the same marketplace (using Cerebro/Xray data — same category, similar price range, similar BSR)
  2. List your ASIN + top 2-3 competitor ASINs (same marketplace — keep all comparisons within UK)
  3. Prompt Claude using Cerebro to compare all ASINs' keyword sets
  4. Claude pulls: every keyword, rank position per ASIN, search volume
  5. Output split into 3 buckets — our advantage, gap/opportunity, shared
  6. From the gap bucket, prioritize high volume + low-medium competition
  7. Ready-to-use list handed off for backend search terms / PPC campaigns

Sample Prompt — Finding Competitors (ASIN + Main Image)

PromptMy ASIN is B0B8DWXLQ2 (UK marketplace). Here is my product's main image [attach image]. Using Helium 10 MCP, find the top 2-3 competitor ASINs in the same marketplace and same category — similar price range and similar BSR. List their ASIN, title, price, BSR, and main image link, so I can visually compare them against my product image.
Note: Helium 10 doesn't do image-recognition matching — it finds "likely" competitors using category, price, and BSR data. You still need to manually check each suggested ASIN's image against yours to confirm it's a true match.

Sample Prompt

PromptUsing Cerebro, pull all ranking keywords for ASIN B0B8DWXLQ2 (UK marketplace) and compare against competitor ASINs B0029MCTJ2 and B0C4XXTS21 in the same marketplace. Show me keywords they rank for that I don't, sorted by search volume descending. Include volume and competition level.

Workflow 2

Rank Tracking & Drop Alerts

Tool: Keyword Tracker

Track daily/weekly rank movement of core tracked keywords and flag drops immediately, before they turn into a sales problem.

Steps

  1. Add top 10-15 core keywords per ASIN to Keyword Tracker (if not already tracking)
  2. Weekly, ask Claude to pull rank history for the last 7 days
  3. Claude compares week-over-week rank change
  4. Set a threshold — e.g. 10+ position drop = flagged as alert
  5. Quick root-cause check: PPC paused? Price change? New competitor entry?
  6. Weekly summary — which keywords dropped, likely reason, likely fix

Sample Prompt

PromptPull rank history for our UK tracked keywords over the last 7 days. Flag any keyword that dropped 10+ positions, and for each flagged one check if PPC spend, price, or listing content changed recently.

Sample Output

motion sensor light outdoorB0912YYP31#4#4—Stable, no action
rgb led controllerB0745MMK22#11#29▼18New competitor entered top 10, 40% lower price

Cadence: run every Monday morning

Workflow 3 · Already in motion

YoY / Periodic Sales Drop Analysis

Tools: Cerebro + Keyword Tracker + Helium 10 MCP

You've already built the 265-ASIN version of this. This turns it into a standard recurring monthly process.

Steps

  1. At month start, export current month vs same month last year sales (Seller/Vendor Central)
  2. Attach the file to Claude: Marketplace, ASIN, Units this year, Units last year
  3. Claude filters "SALES DROP" ASINs using the established rule set
  4. Batch-wise (10-15 ASINs at a time) — pull keyword rank, competitor data, opportunity keywords
  5. Track Helium 10 call budget as you go
  6. Final consolidated table + a "Key Findings" summary (price issue vs keyword issue)
  7. Ready for the team review meeting

Root Cause Rules

Condition (from bulk data)Root Cause Tag
Sessions down + Units down (proportional)Traffic / ranking loss
Sessions stable + Units downConversion loss (listing/price)
Ad spend down sharply + Sessions downPPC stopped / budget exhausted
Everything stable, Units down onlyStock / Buy Box issue

Workflow 4 · Top priority

PPC Optimization

Tool: Adtomic

Feed Adtomic performance data to Claude to spot underperforming campaigns and reallocate budget.

Steps

  1. Export campaign performance from Adtomic: spend, ACoS, sales, impressions, clicks
  2. Ask Claude to identify high-ACoS/low-sales campaigns from the last 30 days
  3. Claude classifies each campaign into cut/increase/watch
  4. Specific action per campaign: bid adjustment %, budget change, negative keywords
  5. Table ready for team approval before execution

Sample Prompt

PromptHere is our last 30 days Adtomic campaign export. Identify campaigns with ACoS above 40% and sales below target, and separately identify efficient campaigns (ACoS under 15%) that are budget-capped. Give me a specific bid/budget action per campaign.

Workflow 5

ACoS Spike Root Cause Analysis

Tool: Adtomic

When one campaign suddenly spikes in ACoS, get an automatic diagnosis instead of manually digging through the console.

Steps

  1. Identify the spiking campaign/ASIN (Adtomic alert or manual scan)
  2. Ask Claude to pull the last 14 days trend: spend, clicks, conversion rate, CPC
  3. Claude checks 4 likely causes — CPC rise, conversion drop, irrelevant search terms, early budget exhaustion
  4. Get one specific fix tied to the actual cause found

Sample Output

CampaignCauseEvidenceFix
UK_Outdoor_Light_AutoIrrelevant search terms34% of spend on "solar garden statue" — 0 conversionsAdd 6 negative keywords, review search term report weekly
Workflow 6

Competitor & Market Monitoring

Tool: Market Tracker

Track competitor price moves and market trend shifts before they eat into your sales.

Steps

  1. Add 3-5 core competitors per category to Market Tracker
  2. Weekly/bi-weekly, ask Claude to pull competitor price and BSR changes (last 7-14 days)
  3. Claude flags significant changes: 10%+ price drop, BSR jump, new listing entry
  4. Quick assessment of impact on our pricing strategy

Workflow 7

Alerts Consolidation

Tool: Helium 10 Alerts

Buy Box loss, hijacks, review drops — instead of chasing separate notifications, get one prioritized daily/weekly digest.

Steps

  1. Confirm notifications are enabled for all ASINs in Helium 10 Alerts
  2. Feed raw alerts to Claude daily/weekly
  3. Claude classifies by priority — Urgent / Medium / Low
  4. Single digest, ready to paste into team chat or email

Step 1

Amazon.co.uk Keyword Search

Where: Amazon.co.uk

Open Amazon.co.uk in the browser and type your target keyword in the search bar.

Steps

  1. Open the Amazon.co.uk site
  2. Type your product keyword in the search bar (e.g. "wall light")
  3. Click Enter — the search results page appears, listing all top ranking products for that keyword
We searched "wall light" on Amazon.co.uk → a search results page with 40+ products opened up.
Step 2

Xray / ASIN Grabber

Tool: Helium 10 browser extension

Stay on the same search results page, then run ASIN Grabber to pull BSR, price, and review data for every listed ASIN.

Steps

  1. Click the Helium 10 extension icon in the browser toolbar
  2. ASIN Grabber automatically overlays a data table on the search results — showing every ASIN's BSR, price, and reviews
  3. Check the BSR (Best Seller Rank) column — the lower the number, the better the product sells
  4. Select 3-5 ASINs that are close to your niche and have a good BSR rank
  5. Note down or export the selected ASINs — these will be used in the next step (Cerebro)

Filters to check

FilterWhat it tells you
BSR (Best Seller Rank)Main filter — low number = high selling, select based on this
PriceOnly consider products close to your price range
ReviewsConfirms the products are established and trusted
Based on BSR rank from the "wall light" search results, we selected 3 ASINs: B0UKASIN01 (BSR #120), B0UKASIN02 (BSR #340), B0UKASIN03 (BSR #510).
Step 3

Cerebro — Keyword Pull

Tool: Cerebro

Use the selected ASINs as competitors in Cerebro to pull every keyword they rank for.

Where to go

Helium 10 dashboard → Tools → Keyword Research → Cerebro

Steps

  1. Open Cerebro
  2. Paste the 3-5 ASINs selected in Step 2 into "+ Add Competitor"
  3. Click Get Cerebro Data
  4. You'll get a full table of all keywords these ASINs rank for
We ran Cerebro with 3 UK ASINs → got 180+ keywords like "wall light", "led wall light indoor", "modern wall sconce".
Step 4

Search Volume Filter — Keyword Selection

Tool: Cerebro results table

Sort by Search Volume High to Low and select the keywords most relevant to your product.

Steps

  1. Click on the Search Volume column — this sorts the results High to Low
  2. Review the high-volume keywords at the top
  3. Tick the checkbox only for relevant keywords (skip irrelevant/brand terms)
  4. The selected keywords are now ready to be added to the final list

Filters

FilterHow to use it
Search VolumeSort High to Low, focus only on the top 20-30 keywords
RelevanceSelect only keywords that directly match your product
After sorting High to Low, "led wall light" (8,400/mo), "wall light indoor" (5,200/mo), "modern wall sconce" (3,100/mo) came up on top — we selected these 20 keywords.
Step 5

Add to List

Tool: Cerebro results table

Save the selected keywords into a centralized list so they can be tracked and shared.

Steps

  1. Confirm the keywords you ticked in Step 4 are still selected
  2. Click "Add to List"
  3. Create a new list (e.g. "Wall Light — Master List") or select an existing one
  4. Save — this list can later also be linked to Keyword Tracker
Why we do this: Keeping these keywords in one centralized list makes it easy to track them, share with the team, and ensures no keyword gets missed.

Sample: We created a new list called "Wall Light — Master List" and added the 20 selected keywords to it — now all keywords are tracked in one place.

Step 6

ChatGPT — New Title / Bullets / Backend Keywords

Tool: ChatGPT

Feed ChatGPT the ASIN details and the Cerebro keyword file to generate optimized listing copy.

Steps

  1. Copy the selected keywords from Cerebro or export as CSV
  2. Prepare your ASIN details — current title, bullets, category, key features
  3. Open ChatGPT and paste both the ASIN details and the Cerebro keyword file
  4. Give the prompt below
  5. Review the output ChatGPT gives — edit if there are any irrelevant/false claims
  6. Paste the final copy into Listing Builder or Scribbles, and confirm all priority keywords are covered

Sample Prompt

PromptUsing these keywords, create an optimized title, 5 bullet points, and backend search terms for this product.
We gave ChatGPT the "Wall Light — Master List" (20 keywords) plus our current ASIN title/bullets — it generated a new title + 5 bullets + 15 backend keywords. We pasted it into Scribbles and confirmed 18/20 keywords were covered.

Amazon Metrics Guide

In funnel order — Metric, Formula, Meaning, Objective

MetricFormulaMeaningObjective
Top — Visibility
Impressions—Count of how many times your product/ad appeared on a customer's screen. No click, no purchase — just visibility.Confirms keyword ranking and reach are healthy.
Clicks—Out of the impressions, how many times a customer clicked in. The next step in the funnel.Checks if the main image, title, and price are attracting attention.
CTRClicks / Impressions x 100Out of 100 impressions, what % turned into a click. Low CTR means the title/image is weak.Measures how strong the listing's hook is.
Mid — Traffic
Sessions—One customer visit to the page = 1 session. Even if the same customer returns multiple times within 24hrs, it still counts as 1 session.Shows how many real customers are actually viewing.
Page Views—Number of times the page loaded. If the same customer refreshes 5 times, that's 5 page views but still 1 session.Helps compare interest levels.
Buy Box %—When other sellers list the same product, they compete for who gets the "Add to Cart" click. The % you're winning.No Buy Box means no sale — direct impact.
Bottom — Conversion
Units Ordered—Total count of pieces/units ordered.Tracks volume.
Conversion RateUnits / Sessions x 100Amazon calls this "Unit Session Percentage". Out of 100 customers who visit, how many actually buy. Most important metric.Reflects listing + price + review health. Good = 10-15%.
Ordered Product SalesUnits x PriceTotal revenue generated in that period.Tracks top-line sales.
Ads — PPC
CPCAd Spend / ClicksThe cost incurred per ad click.Checks bid efficiency.
ACOSAd Spend / Ad Sales x 100What % of ad sales went to ad spend. Lower = more profit.Controls PPC profitability.
TACOSAd Spend / Total Sales x 100Ad cost as a share of total sales. A long-term health metric.Reflects overall business health — matters most long term.
ROASAd Sales / Ad SpendThe return earned per unit spent on ads. Opposite of ACOS.Quick way to check ad returns.
SEO & Health
Organic Rank—The position your product appears at when the keyword is searched (without ads). Page 1 is the target.Confirms whether SEO effort is working.
Search Frequency Rank—Found in Brand Analytics. A smaller number means higher search volume.Helps decide which keywords to target.
Index Check—Whether the product shows up in search for a given keyword. If not indexed, it can't rank.Fixes keywords you're missing out on.
Return RateReturns / Units x 100The % of products returned. High rate signals a quality or expectation problem.A signal of product + listing quality.
